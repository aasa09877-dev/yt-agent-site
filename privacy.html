Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

yt-agent is a personal tool used only by its developer to publish videos to the developer's own YouTube channel. This policy explains what data is handled.

What data is stored

  • OAuth tokens that allow the app to upload videos to the channel owner's own YouTube channel.
  • Video metadata: title, description, tags, upload status and timestamps.

The app does not collect personal data from any other person.

How long data is kept

OAuth tokens are kept until the owner revokes access. Video metadata and metrics are kept for up to 12 months and then deleted.

Sharing

Data is not sold, shared or disclosed to any third party.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. Use of data obtained through YouTube is subject to the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.

How to delete data or revoke access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To request deletion of stored data, email aasa09877@gmail.com.
