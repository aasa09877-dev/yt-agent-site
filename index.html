yt-agent

yt-agent is a personal tool that helps a single YouTube channel owner create and publish short videos to their own channel.

What it does

It generates short vertical videos from scripts, shows a preview to the channel owner for approval, and uploads the approved video to the owner's own YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API.

Who uses it

Only the channel owner (the developer). It is not a public service and does not offer accounts to other users.
